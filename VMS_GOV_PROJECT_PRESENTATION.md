# VMS-GOV Project Presentation

## Audience

Non-technical staff, management, and stakeholders of the Chief Ministry, Southern Province.

## Purpose

Explain why the Vehicle Management System was introduced, the problems it addresses, and how it supports staff, approvers, drivers, and fleet officers during an official journey.

---

## Slide 1: Vehicle Management System

**VMS-GOV**

Digital management of official vehicle requests for the Chief Ministry at Dakshinapaya, Labuduwa, Galle.

- Supports the full journey process from request to completion.
- Brings employees, approvers, drivers, and fleet officers into one system.
- Creates a clear record for every official journey.

---

## Slide 2: The Existing Paper-Based Process

Official vehicle requests traditionally depend on paper forms and manual communication.

- Staff submit forms and wait for updates from several offices.
- Officers must locate papers before they can review or approve a request.
- Vehicle and driver availability may be checked through phone calls or separate records.
- Completed journeys, fuel use, maintenance, and issues can sit in different files or registers.

---

## Slide 3: Problems With the Existing System

The paper process creates avoidable operational risks.

- A requester may not know the current status of a vehicle request.
- Papers can be delayed, misplaced, or difficult to find later.
- Approvals may lack a clear timeline showing who made each decision.
- Manual vehicle and driver allocation can create overlapping bookings.
- Fleet information, licence dates, insurance dates, and maintenance records can be difficult to monitor together.
- Managers spend time collecting information before they can prepare reports or answer questions.

---

## Slide 4: What VMS-GOV Provides

VMS-GOV replaces the paper trail with one shared, role-based system.

- Employees submit and follow their own official transport requests online.
- The correct officer receives each request for review.
- Allocation staff choose a suitable vehicle and driver.
- Final approvers record an explicit approval or rejection.
- Drivers receive their schedule and update the journey when it starts and ends.
- The system stores the journey history for later reference.

---

## Slide 5: A Request From Start to Finish

1. An employee enters the journey purpose, dates, passengers, and route.
2. The appropriate officer reviews the request and records a recommendation.
3. The Deputy Secretary allocates an available vehicle and driver.
4. The Secretary or Senior Deputy Secretary gives the final decision when required.
5. The driver sees the assigned journey and starts it at the scheduled time.
6. The driver completes the journey and records the required meter readings.

The requester can view the request history and its current status throughout the process.

---

## Slide 6: Clear Responsibilities for Each Role

VMS-GOV shows each user only the work that belongs to their role.

| Role | Main activity in the system |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Creates personal vehicle requests and follows their progress. |
| Department Officer | Reviews requests from the assigned department. |
| Deputy Secretary | Recommends eligible requests and allocates vehicles and drivers. |
| Senior Deputy Secretary and Secretary | Carry out the relevant final review and decision. |
| Subject Officer | Maintains vehicle and driver records, fuel, service, and repair information. |
| Driver | Views assigned journeys, starts and completes trips, and reports vehicle issues. |
| System Administrator | Manages users, departments, reusable journeys, and system settings. |

---

## Slide 7: Better Vehicle and Driver Allocation

The system helps allocation staff make practical decisions.

- It shows available vehicles and drivers for the requested time.
- It checks that a vehicle has enough passenger capacity.
- It prevents the same vehicle or driver from being allocated to overlapping active journeys.
- It records reallocations, including the reason and the person who made the change.
- It keeps the requester, driver, and relevant officers informed when an allocation changes.

---

## Slide 8: Support for Drivers and Daily Operations

Drivers can use VMS-GOV as a working schedule.

- View scheduled journeys and assigned vehicle details.
- Start and complete journeys in the system.
- Record odometer readings when the organisation requires them.
- Report a vehicle issue during an active journey.
- Review completed trip history.

This gives operational staff a clearer view of what has happened during each journey.

---

## Slide 9: Fleet Records and Compliance

Fleet officers can maintain important vehicle and driver information in one place.

- Vehicle details, images, capacity, fuel information, service history, and repair history.
- Driver identity, licence details, duty status, and previous journeys.
- Fuel, service, repair, and journey analysis records.
- Revenue-licence and insurance expiry reminders for relevant officers.
- Driver-licence expiry reminders for the driver and relevant officers.

The system sends reminder notifications and SMS messages one month and one week before the relevant expiry date.

---

## Slide 10: Notifications and Communication

VMS-GOV reduces the need to chase updates through calls and paper files.

- In-app notifications show new requests, recommendations, allocations, decisions, cancellations, and issue reports.
- Supported browsers can receive device notifications.
- SMS messages provide an additional alert when the configured SMS service is available.
- Notifications direct each user to the work that needs attention.

---

## Slide 11: Records, Reporting, and Accountability

The system keeps a reliable history of operational decisions.

- Each request retains key dates, decisions, allocation details, and cancellation information.
- Managers can review approved journeys and executive statistics.
- Fleet teams can produce PDF reports for vehicle, driver, fuel, service, repair, and issue records.
- Administrators can manage users and departments, and maintain database backup records.

This improves the ability to answer questions about a journey, a vehicle, or a decision after the event.

---

## Slide 12: Expected Benefits

- Faster handling of official transport requests.
- Clearer visibility for employees and managers.
- Fewer manual checks before allocating a vehicle or driver.
- Better awareness of fleet maintenance and compliance dates.
- A complete history that supports accountability and reporting.
- English, Sinhala, and Tamil user interfaces for wider accessibility.

---

## Suggested Closing Message

VMS-GOV gives the Chief Ministry one practical place to manage official transport. It helps staff request journeys, helps officers make informed decisions, and helps the organisation keep accurate fleet and journey records.
